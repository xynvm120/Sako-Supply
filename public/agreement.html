User Agreement

Effective October 24, 2026. This agreement covers use of the Sako Supply student research prototype.

What accepting means

When you check the agreement box and create an account, you agree to these terms for this research prototype. You agree to protect your login, respect other users, check your order details, and use the available cancellation process. You acknowledge that electronic payments do not transfer real money and that printed receipts are prototype records.

You also acknowledge the Privacy Policy, which explains the account, order and optional driver-location data used by the system. Accepting this agreement does not turn on location sharing; a driver must separately consent and press Start.

If you do not agree, do not create an account or submit an order. You can still browse the public catalog and policy pages.

Research demonstration

Orders and electronic payment outcomes demonstrate system features. Driver tracking displays only locations actively submitted by the assigned device; accuracy and continuity are not guaranteed. They do not create a confirmed commercial sale, transfer money or arrange a real delivery. Printed receipts are prototype records, not official tax invoices.

Your account

Use fictional contact details for testing, choose a password you do not use elsewhere and keep your credentials private. Do not access another person's account, submit malicious content or interfere with the service. Recovery requires identity verification by the administrator and a single-use code; verified accounts can request emailed recovery when the mail service is connected. Admin-assisted recovery remains available.

Products, prices and fulfillment

Products are sold in 25 kg sacks. Listed availability, prices, bulk discounts and zone fees are demonstration data and can change. The backend checks current stock and calculates totals at checkout. Product packaging artwork may be illustrative rather than supplier photography.

Reviewing and authorizing an order

Before confirming, check your rice products, sack counts, phone number, delivery address or pickup choice, delivery fee, bulk discount and total. You are responsible for reviewing the information you enter. The server checks current stock and computes the final amount; availability can change before submission.

Wallet payment previews

GCash and Maya are third-party wallet services. This website is not their official login page and is not claiming an endorsement or merchant connection. The preview lets you approve, cancel or demonstrate a declined payment without transferring funds. Never enter a real MPIN, wallet password or OTP here. A demo approval is not evidence that money was received. Cancelling or closing the preview before approval leaves your cart intact and does not submit an order.

Wholesale minimum and delivery scheduling

The store may set a minimum sack count; mixed rice types count together. Delivery windows use Philippine time and have limited order capacity. Review the selected date and window before confirming. A booking is not a guaranteed arrival time or independently verified delivery commitment.

Unpaid stock reservations

New unpaid Pending orders reserve stock for the timeout shown at checkout. If the store has not started preparing the order before expiry, the server cancels it and releases stock. Orders already being prepared, dispatched, or marked paid do not expire under this rule. Cash-on-delivery remains unpaid until collection and is not cancelled merely for being unpaid during delivery.

Delivery acknowledgment and feedback

Recording a receiver first name and delivery notes requires your separate optional consent at checkout and the receiver’s acknowledgment. You may confirm receipt separately in your order. No signature or photo is required. Feedback is available only for your completed orders, can be edited, and is private to you and the administrator. Do not include sensitive personal information or abusive content.

Orders and cancellation

You can cancel a Pending order through My Orders. Later status changes are managed by the administrator. Reordering uses current stock and prices. Pickup orders do not have a delivery map. As no real electronic payments are collected, the prototype does not process real refunds.

Your rights and policy changes

This agreement does not remove rights that apply under Philippine law. For access, correction or deletion requests and account concerns, use Contact & Help. If these terms change, the effective date and page content should be reviewed again before continued use. The current prototype does not maintain a versioned legal-consent audit.

Acceptable use and availability

Use the system for authorized evaluation. Do not enter real payment credentials. The prototype may be revised, unavailable or contain errors; do not rely on it for actual financial or delivery commitments.

Privacy and questions

Read the Privacy Policy for data handling. Direct questions to Hameem N. Abdulhakim through the school or the administrator who provided the demonstration. See Contact & Help for support.
