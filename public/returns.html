Returns & Refunds Policy

Effective October 24, 2026. This policy describes the Sako Supply research prototype. It does not promise real deliveries or financial refunds.

Cancel a Pending order

Open My Orders → Order details and cancel while the status is Pending. The system restores the reserved stock. Once processing has started, contact the administrator for review; a customer cannot cancel directly at that stage.

Incorrect, missing or damaged items

For demonstration purposes, report the order number, affected rice product and quantity, and a description of the issue through Contact & Help. Photos may be discussed through the administrator's established contact channel; this website does not accept return requests or evidence uploads. Keep any packaging available for review.

Refunds and payment limits

Card, GCash and Maya payments are simulated. No funds are transferred, so the application cannot issue a real financial refund. Order cancellation updates stock and order status; it does not contact a payment provider. Cash records are demonstration entries, not a refund ledger.

Review and resolution

The administrator can review an issue and demonstrate an appropriate order-status change, but the website has no automated returns approval, exchange fulfillment or refund processing. Do not interpret a paid demonstration status as proof of an actual transaction.

Before real commercial use

The store must establish a verified support channel, return eligibility and handling procedures, actual payment reversals, and legally appropriate customer remedies. This prototype policy does not restrict rights that apply to an actual consumer purchase.
