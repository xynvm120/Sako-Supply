Contact & Help

Need assistance with Sako Supply? This is a student research prototype by Hameem N. Abdulhakim.

Contact the project administrator

Contact the researcher through your school or the administrator who provided this demonstration. A public support mailbox has not been configured.

For order questions, provide your order number and a brief description of the problem. Never send passwords, card details or recovery codes in a support request.

Frequently asked questions

How do I order?

Sign in, choose rice in 25 kg sacks, open your bag, select pickup or a delivery zone and complete checkout. Mixed rice types count toward bulk discounts. Watch the ordering guide on the home page.

How do I recover my account?

Select Sign in → Forgot password. If email delivery is connected and your account email has been verified, request an emailed link. Otherwise contact the administrator for identity verification and a single-use recovery code. Codes expire in 15 minutes. Resetting your password signs out your existing sessions.

How do I verify my email?

Sign in and open Your account → Send verification email, then open the emailed link or choose Enter verification code. Email delivery must be configured by the administrator. Existing accounts are not automatically treated as verified.

Where can I find my receipt?

Open My Orders, select your order and choose Print receipt. You can print it or save as PDF using your browser. This prototype receipt is not an official tax invoice.

Can I cancel or return an order?

You can cancel Pending orders in My Orders. For later statuses or product concerns, contact the administrator. Read the Returns & Refunds policy for the prototype's limits.

Why isn't the map showing a real driver?

The administrator must issue a private driver link from the order details. After the order is Out for delivery, the assigned driver opens it on an HTTPS site, agrees to share location and presses Start sharing location. Keep the page visible and allow location access. Until a fix is received the map shows no driver marker; older fixes are marked stale. Pickup orders have no delivery map. Street tiles need internet access.

Was I charged?

Electronic payments are test demonstrations, not real charges. Never enter real financial credentials. Cash collection entries are demonstration records made by the administrator.
