Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026. Sako Supply is an individual student research prototype by Hameem N. Abdulhakim, not a live payment or delivery service. Please use fictional contact information when testing.

Information stored

The system stores your account name, email, salted password hash, phone number and delivery address; orders, item quantities, totals, payment demonstration results and status events; and administrative activity records. Password recovery codes are stored as hashes and expire after 15 minutes.

How information is used

Information supports sign-in, order processing, inventory management, receipts, recovery and project evaluation. Administrators can access customer and order information for these purposes. This prototype does not sell customer information or use advertising trackers.

Payments and tracking

Electronic payments are demonstrations. Never enter actual card or wallet credentials. Raw card fields are not sent to the backend or saved. The delivery map loads street tiles from OpenStreetMap (or MapTiler if configured). The provider receives your IP address and requested map area. An assigned driver can explicitly share device coordinates and accuracy with the order owner and administrator while the order is Out for delivery. The active database stores only the latest fix, not a route history. Stopping sharing, revoking access, completion or cancellation removes the current fix; expired/orphaned or over-24-hour-old fixes are cleaned at startup and every minute while the server runs. Backups may retain earlier copies and require separate removal. GPS accuracy depends on the device and can be spoofed; it is not independently verified. The customer address is not automatically geocoded.

Browser storage and security

A session cookie keeps you signed in. Local browser storage remembers the theme and shopping bag, and the service worker caches public website resources. Private API responses are not cached by the service worker. Account records are held in the server's SQLite database, with local backups. Security measures reduce risk but do not guarantee that all risks are eliminated.

Retention and your choices

The prototype has no automatic account or order deletion schedule. Records may remain in the working database and backups until the project operator removes them. You can edit profile details, sign out and clear browser storage. To request access, correction or deletion, contact the project researcher through your school or the administrator who provided this demonstration. See Contact & Help for the configured support channel. Backup copies may require separate removal.

Account emails

If the operator connects email delivery, your email address and verification or recovery message are sent through Resend, an external email provider. Request these emails only with an address you control. Ownership is verified by a single-use code; unverified accounts require administrator-assisted recovery. Codes are stored hashed and expire after 15 minutes. Email delivery does not enable order emails or SMS.

Before public deployment

A real deployment requires a verified contact channel, defined retention and deletion procedures, and review of applicable Philippine privacy obligations. This notice describes the current research prototype and does not certify legal compliance.
